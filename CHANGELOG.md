# Blackwater Bay change log

Generated from the in-game change log (Options > Change log).

## Version 73 (Oct 15, 2026)

- The controls help screen no longer lists Rum, ship papers. E and A no longer get you off a ship either, and the V key for the horse tonic is gone (use the weapon wheel or inventory). E and A no longer board or hijack ships (F / Y only), and Y no longer uses the horse tonic. Dig, talk and enter stay on E / A.

## Version 72 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Taming a wild horse is now a hold on F / Y (the same button that mounts; E still works). Hijacking: press F, E or Y alongside a weak enemy or merchant ship and the boarding timer counts down by itself, no need to hold the button. Controls condensed. The I key no longer opens the inventory (open it from the weapon wheel). Keyboard: Space and F no longer attack (use the mouse buttons), Q now calls your steed instead of H, and F is the only mount / board / get-off key. Controller: B no longer dismounts or leaves the ship (Y does it), call steed moved from X to D-pad Up, and the D-pad zoom is removed until it is reworked.

## Version 71 (Oct 15, 2026)

- F on keyboard and Y on controller now mount a nearby horse or board a nearby empty ship from foot, and put you back on foot when you press them while riding or at the helm (Q and B still work). With nothing to board, Y still uses a horse tonic. Boarding and hijack fights now use a fixed 50% zoom.

## Version 70 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Ship papers at the Harbour Registry now cost 300 gold (was 150).

## Version 69 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Tutorial hand-off no longer mentions a letter of marque. The dockhand only says the King wants to see a trusted captain; the King grants the commission himself.

## Version 68 (Oct 15, 2026)

- More ships at sea in both modes: roughly 22 merchants (was 12), 11 raiders (was 5) and 9 Navy patrols (was 5) sail the world.

## Version 67 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Controller: the weapon stash menu now keeps your place correctly after each choice (the highlight restarts at the top of the list instead of jumping to a random button), and the highlighted button scrolls into view.

## Version 66 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Controller weapon wheel: hold LB the whole time you use it. Aim with a stick, press A to use the highlighted slice, B to go back a level, and releasing LB closes the wheel without choosing anything.

## Version 65 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Controller: clicking the left stick (L3) now toggles running on and off instead of needing to be held. Running switches off when you let go of the stick.

## Version 64 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Unoccupied ships (docked or abandoned, not yours) can now be hit and sunk by cannon fire, but the harpoon only hooks ships with a crew aboard.

## Version 63 (Oct 15, 2026)

- Fix: in Papers, Please, capturing a ship by harpoon and boarding fight now counts as stealing her. You are returned to the Tortuga pier and the quest moves on to Mercer.

## Version 62 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Text that names controls (toasts, dialogue, quest objectives) now follows the input you are using: keyboard and mouse, Xbox or PlayStation buttons.

## Version 61 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Fix: during Sea Legs the Armory now sells a 10 gold Recruit's Cutlass, so the first weapon is affordable.

## Version 60 (Oct 14, 2026)

- New opening chain in both modes. Washed Ashore: Silas finds you on the beach, you eat at the tavern and tame your first horse. Sea Legs: buy a blade, then Sergeant Brand teaches the weapon wheel, throwing, swinging and the stash (you keep a cutlass and a little gold, the loaned gear goes back). Papers, Please: a stranger at the shipyard lends you a harpoon sloop to steal a ship, sells it, and gives you a free Cutter. First Command: Harbourmaster Pell drills you on the broadside, bow guns, swivel and harpoon, then your ship goes back to stock. After that a dockhand sends you to Captain Flint (single player) or the King (online).

## Version 59 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Walk-in doors: step onto a door to enter a building, step onto the exit to leave it, and the same for stairs in dungeons and the ladder out of a ship's lower deck. E still works.

## Version 58 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Story quests now send you inside Crown Rock castle: the summons, the report and Clear Your Name all continue when you speak to the King in his throne room, not at the door.
- Clear Your Name is longer: an opening scene, a two-level descent (cult-haunted upper crypt, then the lair), Hale's sealed last letter, a brood ambush when you lift his body, Navy patrols on the way home, and a longer scene with the King.

## Version 57 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Single player story: new quest "Clear Your Name". Return to the Gravemaw, recover Lt. Hale's body and carry it to the King, who sees the beast's claw marks, accepts you were telling the truth and grants the Crown's letter of marque. That unlocks privateer contracts at your captain's desk. For now it is started from the debug menu (Grant privateer commission).

## Version 56 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Weapon stash: stored weapons are now grouped by type (Blades, Pistols, Long guns) in sub-menus under the Carrying list (Thrown weapons can be carried or swapped like any other), with their damage shown.

## Version 55 (Oct 14, 2026)

- The captain's desk is now one menu with Ship papers, Repairs and Contracts. File papers to register the ship and unlock the rest; Contracts stay greyed out until you hold the Crown's commission. The separate carpenter's bench and contract board are gone.

## Version 54 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Ship interiors: the captain's desk is only for ship papers and the ship's log. Privateer contracts moved to their own Contract board, and the Carpenter's bench (crew repair) now appears on every ship, including small ones that were missing it.

## Version 53 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Hatchets and throwing knives: after you put one in a hand from the weapon wheel, the wheel offers a melee weapon for your other hand (or keep what you hold). One hand throws while the other swings, and you can still use both together.

## Version 52 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Privateer contracts at the captain's desk (on a ship you own): sink a named raider, or harpoon her and capture the captain. Harder targets pay more, and the tougher ones sail with an escort.
- While a contract runs you hold a letter of marque, so the Navy leaves you alone, but pirate towns attack you on sight. There is a time limit, shown on your objective.
- Single player: the desk unlocks once you hold the Crown's commission (coming later in the story; there is a debug item to grant it for testing).
- Online preview: a short opening contract teaches the basics, then the regular board opens.

## Version 51 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Lumber: a new resource shown in your inventory. Chop trees by swinging a melee weapon at them (2 to 4 lumber each). Trees take several hits: an axe or hatchet is quickest (2 to 3), then a cutlass (4), a rapier or knife is slow, and fists are slowest. A chopped tree leaves a stump and grows back after one full day and night.
- Sunk ships leave floating planks you can collect, and treasure chests often hold a little lumber too.
- Below deck, a carpenter's bench next to the captain's bunk lets you have the crew mend the hull while you sleep. It costs gold and lumber based on the damage.
- Works the same in single player and the online preview.

## Version 50 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Debug menu > Multiplayer (alpha preview): the open world with no story or tavern jobs, on its own separate save. There is no server yet. Use Back to single player on its main menu, or the debug menu, to return.

## Version 49 (Oct 14, 2026)

- Harpoon gun now aims like the swivel cannon: it fires from the bow straight at your mouse, and the aim lines start at the bow too.

## Version 48 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Corral fences now block you on foot as well as horses (go in through the gate).

## Version 47 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Tortuga and Bonny's Rest both fight back after you kill 3 of their people. The fight lasts 45 seconds with a timer on screen.
- During a fight, waves of pirates pour out of the tavern door every 10 seconds. Hold out and you earn gold for every wave.
- The fight ends quickly: when the timer runs out, or if you leave town. Hitting people no longer provokes the town, only kills do.
- Killed townsfolk now slowly return, calm, while you are away.
- The Navy is a bit harder to shake: heat cools slower and starts cooling later.

## Version 46 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Tortuga fights back: attack enough of its pirates and the rest draw swords or pistols for a while. Other towns are unchanged.

## Version 45 (Oct 13, 2026)

- The clean-up sweep runs on its own only (no on-demand button).

## Version 44 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Clean-up sweep: stuck townsfolk are sent home and idle ships you left behind are cleared after a minute out of sight. The last ship you used is never removed, nor are owned ships.
- Far fewer wild horses on the islands and in towns.
- Fenced corrals with cows, pigs, goats and sheep beside each stable.

## Version 43 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Top view: you now walk under the tree canopy (it fades a little when you are beneath it).
- View names are now Top and 3/4 angled.

## Version 42 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Interiors for the tavern, armory, stable, governor, castle throne room, shipyard and Harbour Registry, with beds, meals, a weapon chest and a wardrobe where they belong.
- Ship interior: captain's quarters with a bed, wardrobe and captain's desk, plus a stash and a rum barrel.
- Papers: buy ship papers at the Harbour Registry, then claim a ship at the captain's desk (needs a free fleet slot, up to 3 ships).
- New weapon system: two hands. Attack uses the left hand and alt attack the right. Swap hands from G or the wheel.
- Thrown weapons: grenados, Molotov cocktails (rum bottles), throwing knives (max 20) and hatchets. Out of ammo you punch, or swing your last knife or hatchet.
- Weapon wheel with sub-menus for weapons, items, crew and steed.
- Inventory from the weapon wheel: papers, horse tonic and rum. Rum heals a little, but too much makes you drunk (reversed controls, a sluggish horse, a wobbling sail).
- Custom wardrobe: coat, trim, skin and hat, saved with your game.
- Ships now answer your call near the shore or the nearest dock on your island.
- Bow chaser cannons only swivel a few degrees, and the swivel cannon fires from the same spot at the bow.
- New main menu: Press Start, Continue or New Game, Controls and Options.
- Controls page with Xbox and PlayStation button icons beside the keyboard keys.
- Options page: music and effects volume, screen shake, touch controls, camera view, fullscreen and this change log.
- Tidier pause menu, and shop menus split into categories.

## Version 39 and earlier

- Cloud saves with email sign-in, installable app (PWA), controller and touch support, music and sound volumes.
