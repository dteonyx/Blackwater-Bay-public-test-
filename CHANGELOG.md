# Blackwater Bay change log

Generated from the in-game change log (Options > Change log).

## Version 48 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Corral fences now block you on foot as well as horses (go in through the gate).

## Version 47 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Tortuga and Bonny's Rest both fight back after you kill 3 of their people. The fight lasts 45 seconds with a timer on screen.
- During a fight, waves of pirates pour out of the tavern door every 10 seconds. Hold out and you earn gold for every wave.
- The fight ends quickly: when the timer runs out, or if you leave town. Hitting people no longer provokes the town, only kills do.
- Killed townsfolk now slowly return, calm, while you are away.
- The Navy is a bit harder to shake: heat cools slower and starts cooling later.

## Version 46 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Tortuga fights back: attack enough of its pirates and the rest draw swords or pistols for a while. Other towns are unchanged.

## Version 45 (Oct 13, 2026)

- The clean-up sweep runs on its own only (no on-demand button).

## Version 44 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Clean-up sweep: stuck townsfolk are sent home and idle ships you left behind are cleared after a minute out of sight. The last ship you used is never removed, nor are owned ships.
- Far fewer wild horses on the islands and in towns.
- Fenced corrals with cows, pigs, goats and sheep beside each stable.

## Version 43 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Top view: you now walk under the tree canopy (it fades a little when you are beneath it).
- View names are now Top and 3/4 angled.

## Version 42 (Oct 13, 2026)

- Interiors for the tavern, armory, stable, governor, castle throne room, shipyard and Harbour Registry, with beds, meals, a weapon chest and a wardrobe where they belong.
- Ship interior: captain's quarters with a bed, wardrobe and captain's desk, plus a stash and a rum barrel.
- Papers: buy ship papers at the Harbour Registry, then claim a ship at the captain's desk (needs a free fleet slot, up to 3 ships).
- New weapon system: two hands. Attack uses the left hand and alt attack the right. Swap hands from G or the wheel.
- Thrown weapons: grenados, Molotov cocktails (rum bottles), throwing knives (max 20) and hatchets. Out of ammo you punch, or swing your last knife or hatchet.
- Weapon wheel with sub-menus for weapons, items, crew and steed.
- Inventory on the I key: papers, horse tonic and rum. Rum heals a little, but too much makes you drunk (reversed controls, a sluggish horse, a wobbling sail).
- Custom wardrobe: coat, trim, skin and hat, saved with your game.
- Ships now answer your call near the shore or the nearest dock on your island.
- Bow chaser cannons only swivel a few degrees, and the swivel cannon fires from the same spot at the bow.
- New main menu: Press Start, Continue or New Game, Controls and Options.
- Controls page with Xbox and PlayStation button icons beside the keyboard keys.
- Options page: music and effects volume, screen shake, touch controls, camera view, fullscreen and this change log.
- Tidier pause menu, and shop menus split into categories.

## Version 39 and earlier

- Cloud saves with email sign-in, installable app (PWA), controller and touch support, music and sound volumes.
